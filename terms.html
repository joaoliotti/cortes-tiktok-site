Terms of Service

Last updated — November 18, 2026

Cortes is a single-operator automation pipeline: it watches one Kick livestream, cuts highlight clips, captions them, and posts them to one TikTok account that its operator controls. These terms describe that scope.

01 — Scope

Who this covers

This tool has one operator and connects to one TikTok account, authorized directly by that operator through TikTok's own login screen. It is not distributed to, or used on behalf of, the general public, and these terms are not a consumer agreement for a multi-tenant product.

02 — What the integration does

TikTok Login Kit & Content Posting API

  • Requests a one-time authorization from the operator's TikTok account (Login Kit).
  • Uses the resulting access token solely to upload and publish video clips, either as a draft to the account's TikTok inbox or as a direct post, via the Content Posting API.
  • Never posts, comments, follows, or messages on the operator's behalf outside of publishing the clips described above.
  • Never accesses another TikTok user's account or content.
03 — Content & rights

Source material

Clips are sourced from a Kick livestream the operator has the streamer's authorization to repurpose and publish. The operator is responsible for that underlying authorization; this tool only automates the technical steps of cutting, captioning, and publishing.

No uptime guarantee

This is a personal, self-hosted tool. It is offered with no warranty of availability, accuracy, or fitness for any particular purpose, and may be modified, paused, or shut down at any time by its operator.

05 — Changes

Updates to these terms

If the tool's scope changes — for example, connecting to additional accounts or platforms — this page will be updated accordingly before that change goes live.
