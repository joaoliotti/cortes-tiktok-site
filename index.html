Cortes

A self-hosted tool that watches one Kick livestream, finds the moments chat reacts to, and turns them into captioned vertical clips posted to the operator's own TikTok account.

How it works

Chat volume in, clip out

01Records the livestream in short segments while it's live.
02Watches the public chat and flags moments where message volume or repeated reactions spike.
03Cuts a vertical 9:16 clip around that moment, with a blurred background and the original footage centered.
04Transcribes the clip and burns in word-by-word captions and a title.
05Drafts a title, description, and hashtags from the transcript.
06Publishes to TikTok via the Content Posting API, either as a draft or directly.
Who runs it

One operator, one TikTok account

This is not a multi-tenant app or a public website — it has a single operator who authorizes it against their own TikTok account. There are no end-user sign-ups.
