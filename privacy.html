Privacy Policy

Last updated — November 18, 2026

Cortes is a personal automation tool that turns moments from a Kick livestream into short captioned clips and posts them to the operator's own TikTok account. This page explains what data it touches, why, and where it lives.

01 — What this is

A single-operator tool, not a public service

Cortes runs on hardware owned and operated by the app's developer. It is not a public website or app that other people sign up for — there are no end-user accounts. The only TikTok account it ever connects to is the one the operator explicitly authorizes via TikTok's own OAuth screen.

02 — Data it processes

What passes through the pipeline

No data broker, ad network, or analytics SDK is integrated anywhere in this pipeline.

03 — Third parties involved

Who else sees what

  • TikTok receives the finished video file and its caption text at publish time, under the scopes the operator granted during authorization.
  • Anthropic (Claude API) receives the plain-text transcript of a clip to draft a title, description, and hashtags — never the video file, never chat data, never TikTok tokens.
  • Telegram receives a short text notification (title/description/hashtags) when a clip is ready, sent to the operator's own private chat with their own bot.
  • No other third party receives any data processed by this tool.
04 — Retention & deletion

How long things stick around

Raw stream footage is deleted automatically after a short retention window (by default, two hours). Clips that are discarded (skipped) or already published are kept only as long as the operator chooses to keep them on local disk. TikTok tokens remain stored locally until the operator revokes the app's access from their TikTok account settings.
